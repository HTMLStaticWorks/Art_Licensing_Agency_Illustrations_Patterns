Our Creative Story

About Our Agency

Representing world-class illustrators and surface designers for fabrics, stationery, packaging, and branding campaigns.

Our Agency History

Founded with a commitment to connecting unique illustrator talent with leading retail product manufacturers, Licensing has curated and closed over 120 repeat design licenses.

We focus exclusively on surface design licensing across apparel, fabric print, stationery gift wrap, and beauty cosmetics packaging. This focus ensures our design directors get clean layered source files, precise Pantone colors, and complete copyright clearances for worry-free manufacturing.
