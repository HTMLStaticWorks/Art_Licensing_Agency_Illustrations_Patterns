Textile Sector Collaboration

Anthropologie: Summer Watercolor Apparel

**The Challenge**: Anthropologie sought to license a botanic watercolor capsule for their summer linen lines. They needed complex colorways setup to align perfectly across different fabric screen weights.

**Our Curation**: Licensing matched illustrator Sarah Jenkins. We adjusted color channels for the print mill screens, offset pattern boundaries, and supervised coordinate selections.

**The Outcome**: Delivered 4 coordinate designs. The collection sold out online in three weeks with zero printing errors or layout mismatch issues.

Stationery & Gifting Curation

Minted Co: Whimsical Forest Floor Repeats

**The Challenge**: Minted Co required high-quality woodland illustration spots and coordinating repeating patterns for custom holiday journals and greeting cards.

**Our Curation**: We matched gouache artist Leo Vance, providing layouts designed to adapt cleanly across foil presses and paperboard die-cuts.

**The Outcome**: The whimsical forest wraps launched across national retail locations, increasing custom journal sells by 35% during the holiday campaign.
